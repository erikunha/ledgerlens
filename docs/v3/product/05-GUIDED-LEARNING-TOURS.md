# Eight Public Guided Tours - Scripted Content Blueprint

Each tour uses an authentic captured run or deterministic branch; never simulated model activity marketed as live. Explanations adapt to `beginner / engineer / researcher`, but ground truth never changes.

| Tour | Start case | What visitor learns | Decisive reveal | Time goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Why a refund looks lower | S01 | FX reference vs applied, markup, exact math | one claim verified by sources and numbers | 90 sec |
| Looks like a duplicate | S02 | multiple observations, false positives | 38 sec apart is evidence not categorical fraud | 2 min |
| A deadline is near | S03 | calendar, urgency, escalation | abstention/review due to policy risk | 90 sec |
| Missing transaction | S04 | uncertainty, clarifying questions | no data is not the same as nonexistent world fact | 60 sec |
| Model versus evidence | S09 | injection in a row and SQL/tool boundary | untrusted data denied authority | 2 min |
| Tool outage | S11 | degraded behavior and provenance | partial answer with missing FX | 2 min |
| Cost of correctness | S01 + eval artifacts | A/B/C economics | correct case cost rather than tokens alone | 3 min |
| Many agents or one | benchmark D research | orchestration trade-offs | show paired experimental result or missing test honestly | 3 min |

## Pedagogical sequence

For every tour: (1) case question, (2) prediction prompt, (3) raw data, (4) narrated action sequence with optional exact trace, (5) verification, (6) counterfactual, (7) trade-off/unknown, (8) source and test link, (9) related engineering exercise. The visitor may skip explanations without losing access to evidence.

## LinkedIn content packaging

Tour share card includes `Title`, `problem`, `surprising observation`, `verification artifact`, `tradeoff`, `code link`, `mode label`, `data origin`, `tested version`, `accessibility transcript`. Do not automatically insert numbers not computed from the selected experiment. The share artifact is manually reviewed before publication.
