# ADR-106: Ground improvements in research and test them on project cases

Each improvement identifies the original book or paper that informed its design and explains what the project adopts. Product policies and ledger records determine the expected answers to payment questions.

For a change to the agent, compare the current and candidate versions on the same inputs. Record whether each answer fulfilled the request and whether its factual claims match the evidence. A shorter answer can improve claim precision while omitting the action a merchant needs. Keep both measures visible alongside the answers. [Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments](https://experimentguide.com/) and [FActScore](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/).

See the [dispute deadline evaluation](../cutoff-answer-precision.md) for cases, scores and limitations.
